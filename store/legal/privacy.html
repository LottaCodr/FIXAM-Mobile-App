FixAm Privacy Policy

Effective 19 August 2026 · Host this file at https://fixam.ng/privacy

Who we are

FixAm is a home-services marketplace for Nigeria. Contact hello@fixam.ng.

Data we collect

Account (name, phone, email), job details and address, Flutterwave payment references and masked card last-4, and basic diagnostics. We do not collect advertising IDs and we do not track you across other companies’ apps.

How we use it

To operate bookings, payments, OTPs, fraud prevention and product improvement.

Sharing

Supabase, Flutterwave, and an SMS provider. Artisans see your first name and job address. We do not sell personal data.

Deletion

Delete your account in the app: Profile → Delete account. Email hello@fixam.ng for an export.
